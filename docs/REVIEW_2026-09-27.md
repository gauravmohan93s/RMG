# Pipeline Review — v1.0.0 (2026-09-27)

Scope: full review of the v1 pipeline (tag `v1.0.0`) against the goal *"forecast
application flow for the organisation — scientific, modern, practical, with
evidence of model selection and executive-ready reports"*. Evidence below is from
the code and from the production run `Predictions/2026-09-26`.

## Verdict

The architecture had the right stages, but the forecasts were **not fit for
executive use**. The root cause was structural: models were *selected* on a
3-month, 1–3-step-ahead holdout but *used* for a 12-month horizon, so models whose
errors compound over longer horizons won the tournament.

## Findings (severity ordered)

### Critical
1. **Selection horizon ≠ use horizon** — `evaluator.py` `HOLDOUT_MONTHS=3`; models
   compete only on steps 1–3; chained MoM models explode at steps 6–12.
2. **Bands not tied to the point forecast** — `final_forecast.py::_apply_bands_trajectory`
   compounds ±20%/step from the *last actual*; region bands summed to country
   (quantiles are not additive).
3. **Leakage in backtests**
   - `model09_ensemble.py:138-236` weights chosen on the same window the ensemble is scored on.
   - `model10_global_lgbm.py:230` backtest uses true lagged actuals; production is recursive.
   - `eda.py::_mom_bands` built on full history, consumed inside backtests of models 02/03/04/08.
   - `model03_rollmom.py:183`, `model05_prophet.py:168` bounds computed on full series incl. holdout.
   - Models 01–04/06/07: bidirectional interpolation of the full series before the split.
4. **Month-on-month ratio chaining** (models 02/03/04) — multiplicative compounding
   of per-step ratios is structurally unstable for a 12-step horizon.

### High
5. **Intake summary mislabelled** — `intake_calendar.py` maps the *application
   month* to an intake (April apps → "Summer"), and compares partially forecast
   intakes to full prior-year intakes → false "Contraction/High Growth" alerts.
6. **Policy multipliers are unsourced constants** (`policy_matrix.py`) — no owner,
   date or approval; typo "Centra & Eastern Europe" silently unmatched; the
   Conservative scenario double-counts (low band × headwind).
7. **Evidence bug** — `tracking.py` looks up `Metric/Chosen_Model/MAE` columns that
   `series_selection.csv` does not have → every reason is the generic fallback.
8. **Governance scorecard sorted by band coverage descending** — 100% coverage
   means bands are too wide, not that a model is better.
9. **Missing months interpolated** — the SQL only emits rows with activity, so a
   missing month is a true zero; interpolation invents volume and prevents
   detecting inactive series.
10. **Dead series forecast** — series ending before the global last month get
    12 months of "forecast" starting after *their own* last month.

### Medium / Low
11. The 3-bucket "decathlon" score (40/35/25 weights) has no statistical basis and ~1 point per bucket.
12. Leaderboard column `Series_Won` actually counts series evaluated.
13. SARIMA silently drops series on exceptions (`model06_sarima.py:212,220,270`).
14. Most models produce no prediction intervals; Prophet's are discarded.
15. EDA's capped `prepared_data.parquet` is never consumed (models call `load_data()`).
16. Funnel cap `Submitted ≤ 1.4 × rolling Received` scales bands inconsistently.
17. LLM commentary relies on a 0.5–3B local model with a 4 s timeout; mostly falls back.
18. No automated tests (`test.py` is a diagnostic script); no run manifest (code
    version, data hash, config) → runs are not reproducible or auditable.
19. `Horizon_Months` in the audit store is measured from run date, not last data month.

## Resolution

All findings are addressed in **v2.0.0** — see `docs/ROADMAP.md` (plan and status),
`docs/METHODOLOGY.md` (the science) and `CHANGELOG.md`.
